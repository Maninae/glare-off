    How it works

    1. Find the eyes. A small face finder locates each face and its two eye centers.
    2. Clean the lenses. A straightened strip around the glasses goes through a glare-removal network, which also marks where glare was.
    3. Blend back. Only the marked pixels change, at your photo's full resolution. Every other pixel is left exactly as it was.

    Where glare turned part of an eye pure white, the detail underneath is gone. The tool fills it in plausibly, and tells you when it had to.

    Privacy you can check

    • The page, the two small networks and the runtime come from this site once. After that your browser keeps them and the page works offline.
    • A Content Security Policy tells your browser to refuse any connection to another site, so even a bug could not send your photo anywhere.
    • Downloads have their metadata removed (location, camera, date). The orientation is applied to the pixels so the photo still looks right.
    • No account, no analytics, no ads, no cookies.
